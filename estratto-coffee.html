Our Story

From a farm in
Nariño, Colombia
to your cup.

Estratto began as a simple conviction: great espresso starts long before the machine. Owner Simone Bettini sources green coffee directly from a small farm in the Nariño highlands of Colombia — a region where volcanic soil and high altitude create conditions for extraordinary beans.

We roast in small batches, right here in Vancouver. Nothing is ordered from a warehouse. Every bag has a story. Every cup has a terroir.
